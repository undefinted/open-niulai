# Originality And IP Policy

Open NiuLai extracts a generalized creative grammar: blunt `X来` naming, sincere low-budget 3D execution, strong poster/footage contrast, short repeated dialogue, and a simple emotional mission.

## Allowed

- Original animals, objects, workplace archetypes, abstract concepts, and invented worlds.
- General descriptors such as `sincere broken low-budget 3D meme animation`.
- Transformative commentary that does not reproduce protected expression.
- User-owned or properly licensed reference assets.

## Not Accepted

- Original-film screenshots, footage, logos, exact character designs, copied dialogue, or replicated shot sequences.
- Another franchise's recognizable character dressed as an `X来` protagonist.
- Real brand or celebrity depictions that imply endorsement.
- Prompts asking a model to imitate a living artist's signature style.
- Demo assets with unclear provenance.

## Demo Provenance

Every public demo should record its prompt, generation/editing method, creation date, and confirmation that no protected input media was used. Provider terms and platform rules still apply to generated outputs.

This project policy is a contribution boundary, not legal advice.
